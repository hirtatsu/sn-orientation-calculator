Model. Single β-Sn crystal, in-plane total strain fixed (εxx = εyy = εxy = 0), traction-free along ND (σzz = σxz = σyz = 0). All results are linear in ΔT and given per kelvin; Kτ and the energy do not depend on the sign of ΔT, and stresses are shown for cooling.
